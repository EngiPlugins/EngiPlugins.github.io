    Describe the workflow. If relevant, include the Autodesk product and version.

  2. Explain the steps

    Describe what you do, what happens and the result you expect.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Can I download or buy a plugin now?

Not yet. Our first plugins are in development. The Products page will show released tools when they become available.

Which AutoCAD and Civil 3D versions will be supported?

Supported versions have not been announced. Each released product will identify its tested versions and requirements.

Can I suggest a workflow to automate?

Yes. Describe the task, how often you do it and which steps take the most time. Suggestions help inform development, but do not constitute a commitment to build a particular tool.

Does this website upload my drawings?

No. This website has no drawing-upload facility. If you email us, the information you choose to send is processed as part of the enquiry; see our Privacy Policy.
