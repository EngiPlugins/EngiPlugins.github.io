About EngiPlugins

Engineering experience.
A practical point of view.

Independent development of productivity plugins and automation tools for AutoCAD, Civil 3D and engineering workflows.

The project

Tools begin with a real task.

EngiPlugins is an independent project founded by Yoel Guerrero, an engineer focused on improving technical workflows.

The starting point is the work itself: repetitive drawing tasks, information that needs checking and steps that can be made more consistent.

Our first products are in development. The aim is to turn carefully chosen engineering problems into useful, documented tools.

Development principles

Useful in the work that matters.

01 / PRACTICAL

Start with the workflow

Choose problems that occur in real engineering work and define the result before building a tool.

02 / CONSIDERED

Make behaviour clear

Document how a tool works, check compatibility and explain limitations alongside its features.

03 / LOCAL-FIRST

Keep processing close

Prioritise local processing for future plugins. Any activation, network access or data handling will be described per product.

One project.

Our initial focus is Autodesk CAD and civil engineering workflows. EngiPlugins develops independently and does not represent Autodesk.
