Who is responsible

EngiPlugins is the independent project of Yoel Guerrero. For questions about this website or information sent to the project, contact engiplugins@gmail.com.

Website visits

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, user accounts, contact forms or drawing uploads. Its own code does not set cookies or use browser storage.

The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. This website does not embed third-party videos or remotely hosted fonts.

Email enquiries

If you email EngiPlugins, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are received to handle your enquiry and any necessary follow-up. Please send only the information needed and avoid confidential drawings or client data.

The contact mailbox uses Google Gmail. Messages are handled through that service; see Google’s Privacy Policy. Provider processing may involve countries outside your own. Applicable transfer arrangements must be assessed before this policy is finalised.

For general business enquiries, the proposed legal basis is the legitimate interest in responding to requested correspondence. Where an enquiry concerns steps you request before a contract, the relevant pre-contractual basis may apply. These bases and the actual workflow are subject to owner review.

Retention

The proposed approach is to retain correspondence only while it is needed to resolve the enquiry and any relevant follow-up or applicable legal obligation. A review and deletion procedure must be established before publication; no fixed deletion period is claimed in this draft. GitHub and Google apply their own retention arrangements to data they process independently.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict or object to processing, and receive portable data where applicable. You may contact EngiPlugins to make a request, and may complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority. We may need proportionate information to confirm your identity.

Where processing is based on consent, it may be withdrawn without affecting processing already lawfully performed. The final policy will identify the competent authority and any additional applicable information once the project’s establishment is confirmed.

Future plugins

No EngiPlugins products are currently available. Local-first processing is a development principle, not a statement that every future product will operate without network access. Each released product will describe its actual data handling, including any licensing, activation or telemetry, before use.

Changes and contact

This policy will be reviewed when the website, contact arrangements or products change. Privacy questions can be sent to engiplugins@gmail.com.
